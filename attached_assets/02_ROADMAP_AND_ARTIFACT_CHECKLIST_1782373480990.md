# Portfolio Site Roadmap + Artifact Checklist

## Short Version

Do not try to publish all 35 pages in one uncontrolled pass.

Use Replit to scaffold the full site structure, but publish in phases:

1. Launch-ready core site: home, work index, 6-8 strongest case studies, resume, contact.
2. Expand with the remaining case studies.
3. Add blog drafts, then publish posts only after reviewing them.
4. Add video demos later.

The risk is looking scattered or fake. The fix is to lead with enterprise proof and treat side projects as supporting evidence.

## Recommended Launch Order

### Phase 1 - Replit Scaffold

Goal:
Create the full static site architecture with clean templates, routes, SEO metadata, sitemap, and placeholder slots.

Pages:
- Home
- Work index
- Case studies index
- Systems / architecture
- Blog index
- About
- Contact
- Resume
- 12 case study pages
- 20 blog draft placeholders

Success criteria:
- Site runs locally in Replit.
- Mobile layout works.
- No broken nav.
- No text clipping.
- No fake dates.
- No education/UC Berkeley/MIDS on site pages.

### Phase 2 - Publishable Core

Goal:
Polish only the strongest public pages first.

Priority case studies:
1. Apple Seasonal Search
2. Apple Store AMR
3. CommonSpirit / Dignity Locations Conversion Engine
4. CommonSpirit Medical Content Library
5. Stanford MyHealth SEO
6. ClarityPulse
7. AEO Visibility Infrastructure
8. SearchForge or ActionThread

Success criteria:
- Each page has a real business problem, role, system, cross-functional partners, tools/data, proof, and visual.
- Screenshots are cropped cleanly.
- Claims are defensible in an interview.
- No side project leads over enterprise work.

### Phase 3 - Blog Strategy

Goal:
Create thought-leadership depth without creating fake publication history.

Rules:
- Do not backdate posts as if published if they were not actually published.
- Use draft status or target publication dates until reviewed.
- Publish 3-5 strong posts first, not 20 mediocre posts.

Best first posts:
1. From Traditional SEO to AI Search: What Actually Changed
2. AEO vs GEO vs SEO: A Practical Measurement Framework
3. Citation Velocity: The Metric AI Search Teams Are Missing
4. Entity SEO for AI Retrieval Systems
5. The Enterprise SEO Business Case: How to Get Engineering Prioritized

### Phase 4 - Video + Demos

Goal:
Add proof of actual product thinking.

Future assets:
- 60-90 second ClarityPulse walkthrough
- SearchForge brief generation demo
- ActionThread transcript-to-task demo
- DomainSignal demo
- Date Night product walkthrough

## Missing Decisions

### 1. Primary Job Target

Pick one primary market for the homepage:

- SEO Director / Head of SEO
- AEO/GEO / AI Search Lead
- Technical SEO Product Lead
- Organic Growth Systems Lead
- AI PM hybrid

Recommended:
SEO Director / AEO-GEO / AI Search Systems Lead.

### 2. Website Identity

Pick one:

- Executive portfolio
- Technical product-builder portfolio
- SEO/AEO authority site
- Growth systems operator site

Recommended:
SEO/AEO authority site with technical product-builder proof.

### 3. Side Project Boundaries

Decide which side projects are public now:

Strong:
- DomainSignal
- Date Night
- Vet Advocates

Maybe:
- Cosmetic Watchlist
- Philippine Travel SEO
- Mind Games Archive

Hold unless clearly framed:
- Visionary Strategy Guide

### 4. Blog Truthfulness

Do not fake two years of publication history.

Better:
- Build 20 draft posts.
- Publish the strongest 3-5 first.
- Add “updated” dates going forward.
- If you have older notes, label as “original notes from [month/year], edited for publication.”

### 5. Clarity Digital Claims

Clarify:
- Built and used internally?
- Prototype?
- Representative mockup?
- Client-facing?
- Founder-only workflow?

This matters because skeptical hiring managers will ask.

## Questions You Should Be Asking

- Which pages directly help me get interviews in the next 30 days?
- Which projects create interview risk if I overstate them?
- Which visuals prove enterprise scale versus just personal creativity?
- Am I trying to rank the site in Google now, or convert people who click from resume/LinkedIn?
- What can be safely shown without creating client confidentiality risk?
- Which blog posts should be published, and which should remain drafts?
- What does a hiring manager need to believe after 60 seconds?

## Recommended Next Prompt After Replit First Pass

After Replit builds the scaffold, use this:

Audit this site as a skeptical SEO/AEO hiring manager. Identify anything that feels fake, overclaimed, underexplained, visually sloppy, or distracting from the goal of landing senior SEO/AEO/GEO and AI Search roles. Prioritize fixes that improve recruiter scan value, enterprise credibility, and technical product credibility. Do not flatter the design. Return a page-by-page punch list.
