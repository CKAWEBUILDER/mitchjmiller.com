- Do not mention education on the website. Education belongs only on the resume.
- Do not mention UC Berkeley or MIDS on the website.

- Do not make it look like a founder hype site.

## Tone

Direct, technical, executive-readable, grounded.

No hype. No cringe. No “visionary founder” theater. No decorative AI gradients. No cartoonish personal branding.

## Visual Direction

Make it feel like a polished enterprise SaaS/product portfolio:

- Clean white/light interface with restrained navy, charcoal, soft blue, and one accent color.
- Dense but readable.
- Strong margins and hierarchy.
- Real product/workflow screenshots or credible mockups.
- Cards are okay for project summaries, but avoid a card-heavy marketing page.
- Prioritize recruiter scan value first, then technical depth.

The homepage should work in 10 seconds for a hiring manager.
