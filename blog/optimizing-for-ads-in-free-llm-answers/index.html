Ads in Free LLM Answers: What to Buy, What Gets Cited

Three assistants put ads inside free answers, one uses your chats to target ads elsewhere, and one quit. Click an assistant to see who sees ads there, and why buying the ad still doesn't buy the citation.

By Mitchell Miller · ~7 min read · AI search / paid media · September 24, 2026

Three of the five assistants compared here place ads inside free answers: ChatGPT (Free and Go tiers, self-serve from $25 a day), Google's AI Mode and AI Overviews (through existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns) and Microsoft Copilot (sponsored recommendations). Meta AI uses chats to target ads elsewhere. Perplexity quit ads in February 2026.

TL;DR: three numbers

  • 5.6% of ChatGPT's weekly users pay: 50 million paying subscribers against 900 million weekly users. (OpenAI, via TechCrunch, 27 February 2026.)
  • 25.94% of 50,006 commercial ChatGPT prompts returned an ad; 14.35% of those ads were off-target. (SE Ranking, data collected 23 July 2026.)
  • 11.53%: how often advertisers' own domains appeared among Google AI Mode's cited sources on keywords showing their text ad. The exact ad URL: 1.95%. (SE Ranking, 50,032 keywords, 30 June 2026.)

Can you buy ads inside ChatGPT?

Yes, self-serve, from $25 a day per campaign. Ads have run on the Free and Go ($8 a month) tiers since 9 February 2026, to logged-in adults, US first; the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea followed on 11 August. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu are ad-free (OpenAI, via MacRumors and Help Net Security), and with 50 million paying against 900 million weekly users, more than nine in ten sit on a tier that can carry ads.

Ads are labeled and set apart from the answer. Since 16 September, a Sponsored Agents pilot opens a labeled chat with the brand's own AI agent (US; Wayfair, HubSpot and Shopify named) (OpenAI, via Forkast).

SE Ranking measured ads on 25.94% of commercial prompts, 14.35% of them off-target. An academic audit of 91 test accounts and 3,000+ ads found lower-income accounts received more ads, regardless of race signal (Lurie, Encarnación, Friedler and Metaxa, arXiv, August 2026).

What still earns a citation: ChatGPT cites 4.5 sources per answer, skewed to documentation, papers and vendor pricing and help pages (Orbit Media, 2 September 2026). OpenAI's policy says ads don't influence answers.

How do ads get into Google AI Mode and AI Overviews?

Through campaigns you already run; neither surface can be targeted directly. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns become eligible for AI Overviews automatically; advertisers can't target that placement or opt out, and sensitive verticals are excluded (Google Ads Help). AI Mode takes AI Max, Performance Max, broad match with Smart Bidding, Dynamic Search Ads and Shopping. Since 4 September a small test also admits exact and phrase match "where there is explicit and direct user intent" (Google Ads Liaison Ginny Marvin, via PPC Land). The Gemini app is a separate product with no ads today, though Google SVP Nick Fox told WIRED "we're not ruling them out" (WIRED, 12 March 2026). Google discloses no paid split.

Measured: of 50,032 commercial keywords picked because they trigger text ads, 29.45% showed one in AI Mode on 30 June, more often as cost per click rose (24% under $2, 53.56% at $10+). On those keywords, advertisers' own domains appeared among AI Mode's cited sources 11.53% of the time; the exact ad URL, 1.95% (SE Ranking, 14 July 2026).

What still earns a citation: 54.5% of AI Overview citations also rank organically in top positions (BrightEdge, September 2025). The inference: organic rank, not the ad buy, is the route into the answer.

Does Microsoft Copilot show ads in its answers?

Yes, as labeled sponsored recommendations on commercial queries, tested from late 2024 and generally available in 2025. No Copilot-only campaign type exists; placements draw on existing Microsoft Advertising Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns (secondary reporting; Microsoft documents its ad-buying assistant, not this placement).

Who sees them: consumer Copilot, about 145 million monthly users. The consumer paid count isn't disclosed, and the 28 million paid seats are Microsoft 365 enterprise licences (Microsoft figures, via Panto). The only performance data is Microsoft's own: 73% higher click-through and 16% higher conversion than traditional search, February to May 2025, unaudited (Microsoft Advertising, 6 August 2025). No Copilot citation study turned up.

Can you still advertise on Perplexity?

No. Sponsored follow-up questions ran from 12 November 2024; new advertisers were paused in October 2025 and ads ended in February 2026. Ad revenue in 2024 was about $20,000 of $34 million; an executive told the Financial Times the company is in "the accuracy business" (ALM Corp, 18 February 2026). There is no program and no waitlist.

No one sees ads across its 100M+ monthly users; paid-share estimates (~1% vs 5–7%) contradict each other. The citation is the only door: Perplexity cites 19.2 sources per answer, the most of four assistants tracked, led by LinkedIn, YouTube and Reddit (Orbit Media).

Does Meta AI show ads?

Not inside the chat. Since 16 December 2025, Meta has used Meta AI conversations to personalize content and ads across its apps in most regions, excluding sensitive topics like health (Meta, 1 October 2025); the EU, the UK and South Korea are left out (Proton). A shopping-carousel test inside Meta AI has run since March 2026, US and web only; Meta hasn't confirmed a sponsored label or said whether advertisers get priority (MediaPost).

Reach: 1.2–1.5 billion monthly users by a broad count, about 640 million counting deliberate use; no consumer paid tier is live (DemandSage). No Meta AI placement exists; standard Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp ads are the route, now shaped partly by the chats. No Meta AI citation study turned up.

Does schema markup earn AI citations?

Unresolved. Figures attributed to BrightEdge claim 44% more AI citations for sites with structured data, and 3.2 times as many for 73 sites with proper schema. Ahrefs tested 1,885 pages and found no citation uplift from schema alone. A February 2026 SSRN preprint by Kurt Fischman leans skeptical. None was re-fetched verbatim here. This site argues entity structure helps models understand a brand (entity SEO for AI retrieval); whether markup moves citation rates is a narrower, unsettled claim. Ship schema as hygiene; don't sell it as a citation lever.

How this connects to zero-click

Search Results by Intent mapped how often a results page ends without a click. When the answer keeps the session, the ad moves into the answer: Alphabet earned more than 70% of its 2025 revenue from advertising (Form 10-K), and in Pew's panel just 1% of visits to a page with an AI summary clicked a link inside it (July 2025). Paying puts a brand beside the answer; the citation stays the unpaid route in.

Limits. SE Ranking's numbers are single-date snapshots; Orbit's tracker covers 72 prompts for three B2B brands; OpenAI's figures reach this piece through other outlets. Google changed AI Mode ads twice in twelve days this month (PPC Land, 4 September; Search Engine Watch, 16 September).

FAQ

How much does it cost to advertise in ChatGPT?

The floor is $25 a day per campaign, self-serve, with Persona verification, for businesses in the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or 31 European markets. At $3–5 a click that buys 5–8 clicks a day, too few to judge conversion.

Does advertising in Google AI Mode get my site cited?

Not on the evidence so far: on 50,032 ad-triggering keywords, advertisers' own domains appeared among AI Mode's cited sources 11.53% of the time, the exact ad URL 1.95% (SE Ranking, 30 June 2026). Organic rank is the better-documented route: 54.5% of AI Overview citations also rank in top organic positions (BrightEdge).

Are there ads in Gemini?

Not in the Gemini app today. Google SVP Nick Fox told WIRED in March 2026 "we're not ruling them out." Ads run in Search's AI Mode and AI Overviews, a separate product.

Can I still buy ads on Perplexity?

No. Perplexity ended ads in February 2026 after about $20,000 in 2024 ad revenue. There is no waitlist; the route in is citation, at 19.2 sources per answer (Orbit Media).
