Where the customer is

Mobile is already our strongest channel

Visitors on phones convert five times better than desktop visitors

62% of all clicks come from mobile — from only 24% of impressions

What this tells us

The customer is a visitor with a phone — in a hotel room, or standing on the beach deciding what to do today. When they find SFC, they click. The constraint is not persuasion; it is being found at all. Every point of position gained on mobile is worth roughly five times the same gain on desktop.

11 / 13

Over to you

What we need from the SFC team

Two things that move the needle, and only you can do them

★  Keep hammering for reviews

Hans Hedemann carries over a thousand reviews. Star Beachboys and Kahu each claim more than a thousand. That is a moat built one guest at a time, and it is the strongest signal in local search.

Every lesson should end with a review request — in person, while the guest is still elated and still standing on the beach. Nothing we do on the website substitutes for this.

It lifts map rankings and booking conversion at the same time.

▶  Get busier on social

The GoPro and drone library is a genuine asset sitting unused. No competitor has anything like it.

Posting it consistently builds brand recognition — and brand searches are the one category SFC can win quickly. Recall we sit at position 7.7 for our own name.

It does not need to be polished. Real footage of real guests standing up for the first time, posted regularly, beats anything produced.

Hans Hedemann verified at 4.8 stars / 1,014 reviews. Kahu and Star Beachboys figures are their own published claims.

The horizon

Where this goes

Impressions keep compounding. The specific pages — sunrise, sunset, occasions, groups — reach page one first, because almost nobody is competing for them. The competitive phrases follow, climbing off page four over the months after. Brand searches consolidate as the social library goes to work, and reviews lift both the map listing and the booking rate at once.

The plan does not change. We continue optimising the site and filling it with the right content and the right media, built around what people are actually searching for.

This is slow by nature, and the trajectory is the measure — not any single month's numbers. Right now the trajectory is up.

Questions, and the decision on the paid test, are what we need from you today.
