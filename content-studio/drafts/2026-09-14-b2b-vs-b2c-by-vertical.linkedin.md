## Post

In education, a B2B customer costs 7.3x more to win than a B2C one: $1,143 vs $156, in First Page Sage's 2026 B2B and 2025 B2C reports. The widest gap inside one vertical in this data.

Every vertical has its own number: ecommerce is 1.3x ($86 vs $66), real estate 4.8x. A blended "B2B costs more" average hides that range. (The flashier 13x in this data is B2B education vs B2B wholesale, not B2B vs B2C.)

Second trap: "conversion rate" isn't one metric. The SaaS/software category converts at 3.8% on a landing page (Unbounce) and 7.6% site-wide (Ruler Analytics): two funnel stages, neither split by B2B vs B2C.

I built this as a living artifact instead of a static chart: click one of 10 industries and it opens everything else measured inside it — conversion, paid search, sales-cycle length, email, review reliance — each at its own funnel stage, sourced, with real gaps shown as "no source," never guessed.

The image attached here is the rudimentary version of the living artifact: Education opened, the other nine closed. The interactive one lives on the site: open your own vertical and check the source on every number.

https://mj2.pro/blog/b2b-vs-b2c-by-vertical/

Share this with someone about to build a "B2B vs B2C" slide from one benchmark study that doesn't say which funnel stage it measured.
