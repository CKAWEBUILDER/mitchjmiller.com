**Hook**

One job board says "Growth Operations Engineer" has zero openings in the United States. Another says 10,000+.

Same three words. Same day.

---

**Body**

0 vs 10,000+ for "Growth Operations Engineer." "Director of SEO" returns 19 exact-phrase postings in the entire United States, and zero on Built In — against LinkedIn's 3,000+ for the same words. A factor of 158.
(SimplyHired exact-phrase US search and LinkedIn public guest search, both retrieved 14 September 2026.)

Neither number is a lie. They answer different questions, and nothing on either page tells you which one you are reading.

Published pay disagrees most on the titles with the fewest real postings. Two sources put "Growth Engineer" at $101,752 and $359,824 — 3.5x apart, and the higher one rests on 38 self-reported salaries. The rarer the title, the less its advertised pay means.

In a study of 328,650 marketing postings, the 720 that named AI search, GEO or AEO in the title were 28% leadership roles against 15% for standard SEO titles — and carried a 20.5% median pay premium.
