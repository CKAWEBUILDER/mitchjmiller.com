## Post

*Character count: 1388*

Two job titles show up in half the AI hiring threads I read — and don't officially exist to the U.S. government.

Growth engineer. AI engineer. Neither has a Bureau of Labor Statistics occupation code — no SOC classification, no wage survey, no 2035 growth projection. AI engineer alone out-posts every other title I checked, on every job board.

Statistician and data scientist do have BLS codes, and the real comparison is stranger than the one line most people quote. Data scientist employment is projected to grow 35% by 2035 against statistician's 11% (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, 2025). Data scientists out-earn statisticians nationally, $120,230 to $105,650 — but that ranking flips inside computer systems design specifically, where statisticians hit $167,180 (BLS OOH, May 2025).

I built this as a living artifact, not a static chart: two roads through the same five stops, and a click on any stop on either road jumps to that section of the write-up. Gaps are spelled out, never guessed.

The image attached here is the rudimentary version — a flat copy of the whole map. The real thing is interactive, at the link.

https://mj2.pro/viz/statistician-vs-data-scientist/

Share this with someone choosing between a statistics track and a data-science track — or writing the job description for either.
