Two job titles show up in half the AI hiring threads I read — and don't officially exist to the U.S. government.

Growth engineer. AI engineer. Neither has a Bureau of Labor Statistics occupation code — no SOC classification, no wage survey, no 2035 growth projection. AI engineer alone out-posts every other title I checked, on every job board.

Statistician and data scientist do have BLS codes, and the real comparison is stranger than the one line most people quote. Data scientist employment is projected to grow 35% by 2035 against statistician's 11% (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, 2025). Data scientists out-earn statisticians nationally, $120,230 to $105,650 — but that ranking flips inside computer systems design specifically, where statisticians hit $167,180 (BLS OEWS, May 2025).

I built this as a living artifact, not a static chart: pick any two of the four roles, add an industry lens, rank all four by whichever metric you care about. Every number carries its own source and date — a gap shows as "No data," never a guess.

The image attached here is the rudimentary version — a flat snapshot of one comparison. The real thing is interactive, at the link.
