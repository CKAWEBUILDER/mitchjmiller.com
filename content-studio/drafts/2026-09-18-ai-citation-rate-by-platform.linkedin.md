## Post

Grok's app read 40.7 pages per answer. It linked 0.7.

That's from a new study of 171,264 real chats that users donated from ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and DeepSeek. The researchers logged what each assistant's web search returned and what the answer actually cited.

Share of retrieved pages that became a link, real users:
→ Grok 1.8%
→ ChatGPT 13.3%
→ Claude 19.9%
→ DeepSeek 34.1%

Two more findings stood out:
→ Grok-4.3 on the API linked 25.5%. The app models and prompts differ, but an API test doesn't show you what app users see.
→ 14% to 53% of claims rested on pages the model retrieved and never cited.

The takeaway for anyone measuring AI visibility: a citation tracker only sees the last step. It can't tell "never retrieved" from "retrieved, used and not credited."

The image is the static version of a living artifact. On the site you can switch platform and setting and watch read vs cited re-run, with every number linked to its table.
