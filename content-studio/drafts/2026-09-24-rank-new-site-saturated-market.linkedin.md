## Post

Google showed AI Overviews on 0.01% of local keywords in September 2025, down from 0.14% in March (seoClarity, 500M+ keywords).

It was the first thing I checked in the standard playbook for ranking a new site in a market legacy competitors own. Three premises, three results.

AI answers on local searches: rare on local-intent searches, but Whitespark saw them on 92% of informational local-business searches. Answer those questions plainly.

Long-tail: a way in, not a better closer. In the one peer-reviewed test I found (Ghose and Yang, NYU Stern), each extra word in an organic keyword cut conversion 5.41%.

FAQ schema: not the AI lever. Google's own guide says structured data isn't required for generative AI search, and FAQ rich results left Search on May 7, 2026.

What held up is distribution. Arival's operator survey puts OTAs at 37% of experiences bookings in 2025, and about 22.9 million experiences were booked through Viator alone (Tripadvisor 10-K).

On a 90-day Waikiki surf-school pilot, clicks went from 5 to 54 across two 28-day windows. Small base, one site, and the build I'm proudest of launched after that window closed.

Full write-up, limits included, in the first comment.

Share this with someone about to spend a quarter writing FAQ schema for AI visibility in a local market.
