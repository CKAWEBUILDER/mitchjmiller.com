I went looking for proof that most websites still fail the same technical audit they failed in 2015.

So I picked the population most likely to prove it by contrast: 54 companies selling AEO, GEO and "AI visibility." I expected them spotless, and everyone else to be the mess.

68% passed all ten 2015-era checks on their own homepage. That killed my thesis.

Here is what the data actually said:

18% ship no structured data at all. No JSON-LD, nothing — from companies whose own sales copy sells structured data as the route into AI answers.

46% ship an llms.txt. It is a proposal. No major engine has confirmed it reads it.

86% have never named a single AI crawler in robots.txt. Not GPTBot, not ClaudeBot, not PerplexityBot.

19% returned a 403 to an identified research crawler, while selling crawler visibility.

Adoption tracked how visible and new the artifact was, not how much evidence sat behind it. llms.txt is cheap, legible, and proves currency to a prospect who just read about it. Schema is tedious, invisible, and proves nothing to anyone who is not reading your source.

One caveat I will not bury: silence in robots.txt is not blocking. Unnamed means allowed, which is exactly what these companies want. The 86% is about deliberateness, not misconfiguration.
