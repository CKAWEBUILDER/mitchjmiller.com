For a decade, your Google Business Profile was a visibility asset — win the map pack, get the calls.

In 2026 that quietly broke.

GBP is now an AI grounding source. Google writes an AI "Place Summary" about your business — pulled from your reviews, posts, website, and (critically) your products catalogue — and increasingly THAT paragraph is what a searcher, or an AI assistant acting for them, reads instead of your profile.

The uncomfortable part: a thin profile now gets an unfavorable AI summary regardless of its star rating. Five stars and empty loses to four stars and complete.

So the levers shifted from "post for ranking" to "feed the machine a good answer":

→ Narrow your primary category — biggest single lever (Google changed the taxonomy ~40× in 2025; audit it monthly)
→ Review velocity, not volume — 2–5/week beats a spike (2.8× CTR lift); reply within 24h
→ Populate the products catalogue — now a DIRECT input to AI answers, and most businesses leave it empty
→ Seed 10–15 real FAQs — grounding text for the summary
→ Weekly posts + fresh photos — +42% directions, +35% clicks

And the risk surface hardened: video verification is now the default, and an AI-content policy auto-enforces against fake reviews, photos, and awards.

Treat your profile less like a listing you set and forget — and more like the canonical dataset an AI reads about you every day. Feed it well.
