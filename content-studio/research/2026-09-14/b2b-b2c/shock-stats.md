# Shock-Stat Shortlist — B2B vs B2C

Tile-ready sentences for the interactive artifact. Confidence: **High** = directly fetched from
named primary publisher; **Medium** = clear named source via search summary, not independently
fetched; **Low** = aggregator/secondary source, or figures disagree across sources. Full sourcing
in `data.json` / `sources.md`.

1. **A B2B software landing page converts at 3.8% — barely more than half the 6.6% all-industry
   median. Events and entertainment pages convert at 12.3%, more than 3x higher.**
   Source: Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report (2024 data, 41,000 pages / 464M visitors).
   Confidence: Medium (SaaS + median figures confirmed; 12.3% figure via secondary aggregator).

2. **Educating a B2B customer costs $1,143 in acquisition spend. Acquiring a B2C ecommerce
   customer costs $86. That's a 13x gap — for functionally the same job, "get someone to buy."**
   Source: First Page Sage, B2B and B2C CAC by Industry reports (2026 / 2025).
   Confidence: High (both reports directly fetched, matched methodology).

3. **B2B buyers spend just 17% of their total purchase time talking to a vendor. The other 83%
   is spent researching alone — reading, comparing, asking peers — before a salesperson ever
   gets a meeting.**
   Source: Gartner (2024 research, cited across multiple 2026 B2B buying-behavior reports).
   Confidence: Medium (widely and consistently cited, not independently fetched from gartner.com).

4. **92% of B2B software buyers say they consult peer reviews before they buy. That's
   statistically indistinguishable from the 93–96% of consumers who read reviews before an
   Amazon purchase. Reviews aren't a B2C thing anymore — they're a buying-decision thing.**
   Source: G2 2024 Buyer Behavior Report (B2B); consumer-review roundup, multiple 2026 studies (B2C).
   Confidence: Medium.

5. **A cold click from ChatGPT or Claude converts at up to 5x the rate of a click from Google
   organic search (14–17% vs. 2.8%) on ecommerce sites — but this needs primary-source
   confirmation before you build a headline around it.**
   Source: cited near a working paper analyzing 973 ecommerce sites / $20B revenue (Aug 2024–Jul 2025).
   Confidence: Low — attribution to that specific paper not fully confirmed; verify before publishing.

6. **The starkest CAC gap in the entire dataset: B2B eCommerce (wholesale/ordering platforms)
   acquires a customer for $86. B2B Education acquires one for $1,143. Same "B2B" label, a 13x
   spread — the label tells you almost nothing without the vertical.**
   Source: First Page Sage B2B CAC by Industry (2026).
   Confidence: High (directly fetched).

7. **A real estate website converts visitors to leads at 4.7% — but only 0.4–1.2% of those
   leads ever close. A referral from someone's personal network closes at 15–25%. The channel
   matters more than the traffic volume.**
   Source: Promodo 2026 Real Estate Marketing Benchmarks; ConversionRealtor 2026 Benchmark Report.
   Confidence: Medium (two different aggregator sources, consistent directionally).

8. **Nonprofit fundraising emails get a 0.59% click rate. Nonprofit advocacy emails — asking for
   an action, not a donation — get 2.3%, nearly 4x higher. Asking for money is the single
   hardest click to earn in email marketing.**
   Source: M+R Benchmarks 2026.
   Confidence: Medium (named annual industry-standard report, not independently fetched).

9. **B2B marketers spend 82% of their Google Ads budget on non-branded keywords — and get a
   68% return. The other 18%, spent on their own brand name, returns 1,299%. Nineteen times the
   ROI, on the smaller slice of the budget.**
   Source: Dreamdata, B2B branded vs. non-branded search analysis (2026).
   Confidence: Medium.

10. **A B2B attorney's cost-per-lead on Google Ads is $131.63. A pet-industry advertiser's is
    $31.50 — a 4x gap driven almost entirely by average CPC ($9.87 vs. $4.06), not by clicks or
    conversion rate.**
    Source: WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 Google Ads Benchmarks (13,000+ campaigns, Apr 2025–Mar 2026).
    Confidence: High (table cross-verified against publisher's own topline averages).

11. **A $100K+ B2B deal takes an estimated 417 touchpoints to close. A consumer purchase takes
    about 6. Every number in between (27, 60, 76) has been published as "the" B2B touchpoint
    count by a different source — the real finding is the spread itself, not any single number.**
    Source: multiple aggregators (HockeyStack Labs, Dreamdata, Geisheker), 2026.
    Confidence: Low on any single figure; High on the qualitative gap.

12. **Mailchimp's own numbers: nonprofit emails open at 40%, ecommerce emails open at 30% — the
    widest gap of any two categories on their published benchmark page, from the same platform,
    same measurement method, same month.**
    Source: Mailchimp Email Marketing Benchmarks (data as of Dec 2023, live page retrieved Sep 2026).
    Confidence: High (directly fetched from publisher).
