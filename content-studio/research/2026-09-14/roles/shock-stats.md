# Shock-stat shortlist — statistician vs data scientist (vs growth engineer / AI engineer)
Compiled 2026-09-14. Tile-ready sentences, one stat each, source + confidence noted. All figures traceable to data.json / sources.md.

1. **Data scientist employment is projected to grow 35% from 2025–2035 — more than 3x the 11% projected for statisticians** in the same window. (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, 2025 edition, modified 2026-08-27) — **Confidence: High**

2. **Data scientists out-earn statisticians by ~$14,600 a year at the median** ($120,230 vs $105,650, May 2025) — but the ranking flips inside computer systems design, where statisticians hit a $167,180 median, ~$35K above data scientists in the same industry ($132,380). (BLS OEWS / OOH) — **Confidence: High**

3. **On Wellfound's startup board, AI engineer listings outnumber data scientist listings 1,985 to 161 — a 12.3x gap**, both remote-only, single-day snapshot. (Wellfound, accessed 2026-09-14) — **Confidence: High** (live snapshot, will drift)

4. **62% of statisticians hold a master's degree as their terminal credential; data scientists split 48% bachelor's / 44% master's** — a full degree-tier gap between the two fields' typical entry bar. (O*NET OnLine 15-2041.00 / 15-2051.00) — **Confidence: High**

5. **"Growth engineer" and "AI engineer" have no Bureau of Labor Statistics occupation code at all** — no SOC classification, no wage survey, no OOH page — even though AI engineer beats every other title studied for job-board volume on Indeed, Built In, and Wellfound alike. (BLS SOC lookup guidance; job-board snapshot 2026-09-14) — **Confidence: High**

6. **AI-skilled workers commanded a 56% wage premium in 2025 — more than double the 25% premium measured a year earlier.** (PwC 2025 Global AI Jobs Barometer, ~1B job ads analyzed, June 2025) — **Confidence: Medium-High** (direct PDF fetch blocked; figure corroborated across 4 independent PwC regional pages)

7. **AI professionals command a 9–13% cash premium over data scientists at the same seniority level**, based on a 2025 survey of 866 working practitioners — the gap widest where scarce Gen-AI expertise adds the most value. (Burtch Works 2025 AI & Data Science Compensation Report) — **Confidence: High**

8. **Federal government is the single largest employer of statisticians (16% of the workforce)**; data scientists concentrate instead in publishing/media (12%), insurance (10%) and corporate management (10%) — almost no overlap in institutional home. (BLS OOH, Work Environment) — **Confidence: High**

9. **Python usage jumped 7 percentage points in one year (2024→2025) to 57.9% of all developers** — Stack Overflow's largest single-year language swing, credited to AI and data-science adoption. (2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey, n=31,771) — **Confidence: High**

10. **Kaggle hasn't published an official "State of Data Science" report since 2022** — the field's own most-cited community benchmark has gone dark for 3+ years even as the roles it tracks keep splitting into new titles. (Kaggle) — **Confidence: High** (on the gap itself)

11. **Data scientist median pay swings nearly 2x depending on which 2025 survey you read**: Stack Overflow's global sample says $82,910; a US-only cut of that same dataset says $160,000; BLS's national wage survey lands in between at $120,230. (Stack Overflow 2025 / techrecruiting.io / BLS OEWS) — **Confidence: Medium** (illustrates measurement disagreement, not a single fact)

12. **Computer and Information Research Scientists — the closest BLS-recognized cousin to "AI engineer" — are projected to grow 22% through 2035, doubling the 11% projected for statisticians but still trailing data scientists' 35%.** A three-point growth ladder in one BLS dataset. (BLS OOH) — **Confidence: High**
