# Shock-Stat Shortlist — Search Results by Intent
Researched 2026-09-14. Each line is written tile-ready (standalone, no context needed). Full sourcing in `sources.md` and `data.json`.

1. **68.01% of U.S. Google searches ended with zero clicks in early 2026 — up from 60.45% just two years earlier.**
   Source: SparkToro (Rand Fishkin), using Similarweb clickstream data, Jan–Apr 2026, published 2026-06-09. Confidence: **High**.

2. **When an AI Overview appears, the #1 organic result's click-through rate drops 58% — and that "AI tax" has nearly doubled in eight months (it was 34.5% in April 2025).**
   Source: Ahrefs (Ryan Law & Xibeijia Guan), 300,000 keywords via Google Search Console, Dec 2025 data, published 2026-02-04 (update to an April 2025 study). Confidence: **High**.

3. **AI Overviews were 91.3% informational queries in January 2025. By October 2025, that was down to 57.1% — AI Overviews have moved into commercial, transactional, and navigational territory almost as fast as they conquered "how to" searches.**
   Source: Semrush, 10M+ keywords tracked through 2025, published 2026-02-02. Confidence: **High**.

4. **"X vs Y" comparison searches are the single most AI-Overview-saturated query type measured anywhere: 95.4% trigger one.**
   Source: Seer Interactive, 280 comparison queries within a 5.47M-query study, Jan 2025–Feb 2026, published 2026-04-24. Confidence: **High**.

5. **Show up in an AI Overview but don't get cited, and your click-through rate falls to 0.94% — a 3.6x drop from the 3.35% you'd get with no AI Overview on the page at all.**
   Source: Seer Interactive, 5.47M tracked queries / 53 brands / 2.43B impressions, published 2026-04-24. Confidence: **High**.

6. **AI Overviews on commercial-intent searches grew 71% in six months — while AI Overviews on transactional (ready-to-buy) searches actually fell 5% over the same stretch. Google is chasing comparison shoppers, not checkout pages.**
   Source: Semrush, 600,000+ keywords / 10 industries, Nov 2025–Apr 2026, published 2026-07-02. Confidence: **High**.

7. **SERPs showing both a Google Ad and an AI Overview grew 394% in 2025 — from 5.17% of AI-Overview pages in March to 25.56% by October.**
   Source: Semrush, 10M+ keywords, published 2026-02-02. Confidence: **High**.

8. **Paid ads' share of clicks on commercial searches more than doubled in one year: in the headphones category, combined paid click share went from 16% to 36% (Jan 2025→Jan 2026) as organic fell from 73% to 50%.**
   Source: Search Engine Land / Aleyda Solis, analysis of Similarweb SERP-composition data across 4 categories, published 2026-02-18. Confidence: **High**.

9. **Local search is the one place AI Overviews haven't taken over: only 15% of local-intent queries trigger one, versus 92% for informational queries in the same study — but a Local Pack still shows up on 93% of local-intent searches.**
   Source: Whitespark, 540 queries / 3 U.S. metros / 6 verticals, manual collection, published 2025-05-12. Confidence: **High**.

10. **Navigational searches — "chase bank login," "youtube" — showed a 0% AI Overview trigger rate in a late-2024 study. By October 2025, a separate large-sample study put navigational AI Overview presence at 10.33%. Even "just take me to the site" searches aren't AI-immune anymore.**
    Source: Authoritas (Dec 2024, published 2025-01) and Semrush (Oct 2025 reading, published 2026-02-02) — two studies, same directional finding. Confidence: **High**.

11. **Featured snippets — Google's old "position zero" — nearly disappeared in six months: present on 15.41% of desktop SERPs in January 2025, just 5.53% by June (-64%), as AI Overviews took the slot instead.**
    Source: Ahrefs, 1M randomly sampled U.S. desktop SERPs, 2025 (exact post URL not independently re-verified this pass — see notes.md). Confidence: **Medium**.

12. **Reddit's share of ChatGPT's citations collapsed from ~60% to ~10% in six weeks after a September 2025 platform change — the fastest visibility swing measured across any AI platform this year.**
    Source: Semrush, 230,000+ prompts / 100M+ citations tracked weekly, Jul–Oct 2025, published 2025-11-10. Confidence: **High**.
