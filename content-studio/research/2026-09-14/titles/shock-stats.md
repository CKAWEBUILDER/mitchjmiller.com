# Shock-Stat Shortlist — Job-Title Market Research
Retrieved 2026-09-14. Each line is tile-ready: sentence, number, source, date, confidence.

1. **The exact phrase "growth operations engineer" returns zero results on SimplyHired's US job search** — "We could not find any 'growth operations engineer' jobs in United States" — while LinkedIn's own keyword search claims "10,000+" for the same term. (SimplyHired, https://www.simplyhired.com/search?q=%22growth+operations+engineer%22&l=United+States, retrieved 2026-09-14; LinkedIn, https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search?keywords=growth%20operations%20engineer&location=United%20States, retrieved 2026-09-14). **Confidence: high** (both numbers directly observed; the gap itself, not either number alone, is the finding).

2. **GTM Engineer postings jumped from ~1,400 in mid-2025 to 3,000+ by January 2026 — a 205% year-over-year surge, and roughly 13x faster growth than the broader tech labor market.** (GTME Pulse / Bloomberry job-market analysis, https://gtmepulse.com/insights/job-market-2026/, 2026). **Confidence: medium** (trade-publisher synthesis of a named 1,000-posting study, not a primary government dataset).

3. **A 1,000-job study of GTM Engineer postings found SQL and Python each appear in 38% of listings, and the single most-cited tool is Clay — ahead of HubSpot (52%), Outreach (49%), Salesforce (45%), Zapier (39%), Apollo (29%), and n8n (28%).** (Bloomberry, "I analyzed 1000 GTM Engineering jobs," https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-1000-gtm-engineering-jobs-here-is-what-i-learned/, published 2025-10-03, updated 2026-01-25). **Confidence: high** (named, dated, methodology-disclosed primary study).

4. **Top GTM Engineer total-comp offers reach $252,000 at Vercel and $250,000 at OpenAI — while the median across all postings is just $127,500, a 2x spread between headline and median pay.** (Bloomberry, same study as above). **Confidence: high**.

5. **A study of 328,650 marketing job postings (of 632,840 collected) found 720 postings in the last 12 months carry "AI search," "GEO," or "AEO" directly in the job title — and 28% of those are leadership roles (Director/Head/VP/Principal/Lead), nearly double the 15% leadership rate for standard SEO titles.** (SearchForHire, "The Future of Search: 5 Experts and 328,650 Job Postings," https://www.searchforhire.com/blog/the-future-of-search-5-experts-and-328650-job-postings-on-what-happens-next/, published 2026-07-15). **Confidence: high** (largest, most methodologically transparent study in this research set).

6. **SEO job ads mentioning AI-search/GEO/AEO skills nearly doubled in six months — from 12.2% of postings in December 2025 to 20.3% in June 2026 — and those postings pay a median $117,500 vs. $97,500 for ones that don't, a 20.5% premium.** (SearchForHire, same study, 2026-07-15). **Confidence: high**.

7. **"Director of SEO" returns 0 results on Built In and just 19 on SimplyHired's exact-phrase US search — versus LinkedIn's generic "3,000+" for the same loose keyword match.** (Built In, https://builtin.com/jobs?search=director+of+seo; SimplyHired, https://www.simplyhired.com/search?q=%22director+of+seo%22&l=United+States; LinkedIn, https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search?keywords=director%20of%20seo&location=United%20States; all retrieved 2026-09-14). **Confidence: high**.

8. **Glassdoor's average "Growth Engineer" salary — $359,824/year — is more than 3x ZipRecruiter's figure for the identical title ($101,752/year), and Glassdoor's own sample is just 38 self-reported salaries.** (Glassdoor, https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/growth-engineer-salary-SRCH_KO0,15.htm, 2026-05; ZipRecruiter, https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Growth-Engineer-Salary, 2026-07). **Confidence: high** (both numbers directly observed; illustrates how unstable "the salary" is for an unstandardized title).

9. **50+ named companies — including Stripe, Amazon, Pfizer, LinkedIn, Victoria's Secret, eBay, Palo Alto Networks, HubSpot, and Anthropic — now carry a dedicated AEO/AI-search role, spanning finance, healthcare, retail, and enterprise tech, not just SaaS marketing teams.** (Kaleigh Moore, "AEO Job Openings in 2026," https://www.kaleighmoore.com/blog/2026/7/6/ai-search-jobs, published 2026-07-06). **Confidence: high**.

10. **94% of surveyed CMOs and digital leaders plan to increase AEO/GEO investment in 2026, and 97% already reported a measurable positive impact from it in 2025.** (Conductor survey of 250+ CMOs, cited via Sloane Staffing, https://www.sloane-staffing.com/insights/aeo-geo-hiring-2026/, published 2026-08-11). **Confidence: medium** (self-reported survey of marketing leaders, not a hiring/postings dataset).

11. **RevOps job postings reportedly grew 127% year-over-year (Q1 2025 → Q1 2026), with RevOps headcount growing 3.2x faster than all other go-to-market roles combined.** (CRM Today, "RevOps Is Now the Fastest-Growing Role in B2B Tech," https://crmtoday.news/industry/revops-fastest-growing-role, 2026). **Confidence: low-medium** (trade-blog claim; could not cross-verify against a primary jobs dataset in this session — flag before using as a headline number).

12. **A fully-loaded growth engineering team costs roughly $1 million a year and typically isn't justified below $5 million in recurring revenue — the discipline traces directly to Facebook's 2007 growth team under Chamath Palihapitiya.** (Pragmatic Engineer, "What is Growth Engineering?", https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/what-is-growth-engineering, published 2025-03-20). **Confidence: high** (named primary interview source, exact date confirmed).

---
**Best cross-title methodology tile (meta-stat, not title-specific):** Across every title tested, LinkedIn's public guest-search count and SimplyHired's exact-phrase count disagree by one to four orders of magnitude — e.g. "Growth Data Engineer" shows 1 result on SimplyHired vs. LinkedIn's "11,000+"; "Revenue Operations Engineer" shows 1 vs. "6,000+." This is not a fluke of one board; it held across all 10 titles tested and is arguably the single most shareable finding in this dataset — a visual on "how many job postings really exist" needs to show methodology, not just a number.
