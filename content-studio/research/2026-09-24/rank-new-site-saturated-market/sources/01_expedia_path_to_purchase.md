# Expedia Group — The Path to Purchase: Uncovering How Travelers Plan and Book
URL: https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/resources/blog/path-to-purchase-insights
Publisher: Expedia Group (Media Solutions / partner resources)
Date: 2024-01-09 | Fetched live: 2026-09-24 (HTTP 200)
Method: digital tracking data from 70,000+ travelers + survey of 5,700+ respondents; AU, CA, FR, JP, MX, UK, US
Type: first-party clickstream panel + survey (industry primary)

VERBATIM:
- "travelers view 141 pages of travel content in the 45 days prior to booking a trip"
- "consuming, on average, just over five hours of content (303 minutes)"
- "This number is as high as 524 minutes, or 8 hours and 44 minutes, in the U.S."
- "80% of travelers visiting an OTA at some point before making a travel purchase"
- "59% of travelers were undecided on a destination when they first decided to take a trip"
- "51% of travelers booked on an OTA, 37% booked on an airline website or app, and 23% booked on a hotel website or app"
Resource usage: search engines 61%, social media 58%, airline sites 54%

CAVEAT: measures PAGES of content, not discrete "touchpoints"/brands. Covers travel broadly (flights/hotels), NOT tours & activities specifically.
