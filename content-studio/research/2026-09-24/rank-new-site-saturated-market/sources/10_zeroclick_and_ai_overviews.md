# Zero-click search + AI Overviews prevalence (verified 2026-09-24)

## STRONGEST METHODOLOGY: Pew Research Center
URL: https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/
Date: 2025-07-22 | Data: March 1-31, 2025
Method: 900 US adults, KnowledgePanel Digital, browser-tracking app; 68,879 real searches, 12,593 with AI summary
VERBATIM:
- "Users who encountered an AI summary clicked on a traditional search result link in 8% of all visits."
- "Around one-in-five Google searches in March 2025 produced an AI summary"
Without AI summary: 15% click rate. Session abandonment 26% (AIO) vs 16% (no AIO).
CLASS: PRIMARY-VERIFIED, non-commercial, probability-based panel. Best in the set.

## SparkToro 2026 zero-click
URL: https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/
Date: 2026-06-09 | Data: Jan-Apr 2026, US
VERBATIM: "In the first four months of 2026, a whopping 68.01% of Google searches ended without a click."
Method: Similarweb desktop+mobile web panel (US). Mobile/desktop weighting is an EXTERNAL ASSUMPTION ("2/3rds mobile, 1/3rd desktop"), not panel-measured. Excludes Google mobile APP.
CLASS: PRIMARY-VERIFIED
WARNING: SparkToro's 2024 study says 58.5% US; the 2026 post restates 2024 as 60.45%. Both live, they disagree. Panel changed Datos -> Similarweb. DO NOT draw a "58.5% -> 68%" trend line.

## Semrush AI Overviews (largest disclosed longitudinal)
URL: https://www.semrush.com/blog/semrush-ai-overviews-study/
Date: refreshed 2025-12-15 | Data Jan-Nov 2025 | 10M+ keywords + Datos clickstream
VERBATIM: "AI Overviews were triggered for 15.69% of queries in November."
COUNTER-EVIDENCE (rarely cited): on matched keywords "the zero-click rate decreased from 33.75% to 31.53%."

## seoClarity (largest keyword universe)
URL: https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact
Date: updated 2025-10-23 | 500M+ keyword US dataset
VERBATIM: "AIOs now appear for 30% of U.S. desktop keywords-a new high (as of September 2025)."
LOCAL: "AIOs had begun appearing for a meager 0.14% of local keywords in March 2025. Now, in September 2025, this number has fallen to just 0.01%."

## LOCAL INTENT SUPPRESSES AI OVERVIEWS -- the load-bearing finding
- seoClarity: local keywords 0.01% AIO (Sept 2025)
- Whitespark: https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/ (2025-05-12)
  LOCAL-INTENT queries ~15% AIO vs informational 92%. Sample only 540 queries, 3 cities, 6 industries, manual.
  TRAP: Whitespark's headline "68% of local business queries" is their MIXED set. The local-intent number is 15%.
- Semrush: lowest-AIO industries lean local intent.

## THE METHODOLOGY POINT THAT MATTERS
The 12%-48% AI Overview range is NOT disagreement about reality -- it is keyword-set composition.
Per-SEARCH claim -> Pew ~18%. Per-KEYWORD claims -> Semrush 15.69% / seoClarity 30% / BrightEdge 48%.
