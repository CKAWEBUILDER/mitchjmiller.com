## What the demand and competition support
- **Favored 1: an AI-visibility plus local audit, fixed fee, written handoff.** Audit, price and Hawaii-qualified long-tail SERPs are held by small agencies, how-to blogs and templated city pages, not tool vendors or listicles, and M² has first-party proof they lack. Rivals sell it too: SEMhawaii pitches an enterprise background, AI search and a free Honolulu audit ([home](https://semhawaii.com/), [audit](https://semhawaii.com/locations/honolulu-seo/)); Ehukai Media pitches software plus technical SEO ([site](https://ehukaimedia.com/)). Lead on proof and the handoff, not on "free". Anchors, unverified and taken from search summaries: AEO audits about $400–$1,500, RankWorks from $1,200 ([RankWorks](https://rankworks.com/best-answer-engine-optimization-services/)); small-business SEO audits $500–$2,500 ([Expert Market](https://www.expertmarket.com/seo-services/seo-audit-cost)).
- **Favored 2: monthly SEO plus AI-visibility management with agent-assisted implementation, sold as an upsell from the audit** (not search-led). Anchors, unverified and taken from search summaries: SEO retainers $675–$3,500+ a month ([Studio Blue](https://studiobluecreative.com/2026/09/24/seo-retainer-cost-small-business/)), small-business AEO retainers $1,500–$5,000 ([Solutionarian](https://solutionarianmarketing.com/blog/search-engine-optimization/how-much-does-answer-engine-optimization-cost-aeo-pricing-guide-for-small-businesses-2026/)). SaaS agents list at $99 a month ([Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/seo-ai-agent), [QuQi](https://www.quqi.io/ai-marketing/small-business)) and from $39.99 (KIVA, per a summary), so "an agent team" cannot be the price driver; price managed outcomes and oversight.
- **Favored 3: strategy session plus handoff.** The DIY-audit SERP is nine how-to guides with no packaged offer ([Tabula](https://tabula.agency/blog/diy-seo-audit-small-business/)); the consultant SERP is individuals.
- **Later: B2B and fractional SEO for larger clients.** Credentials fit (Apple, Stanford, CommonSpirit) but the SERPs belong to personal brands ([Bill Widmer](https://billwidmer.com/best-b2b-saas-seo-consultants/), [Trevor Fox](https://trevorfox.com/fractional-seo/)); keep it LinkedIn and referral led.
- **Argues against, for now:** standalone web design/dev (local firms and directories, [Expertise.com](https://www.expertise.com/business/web-design/hawaii/honolulu)); standalone GBP management at $100–$500 a month ([Merchynt](https://www.merchynt.com/post/google-my-business-management-pricing)); prediction models as a lead service (consultancies, [Vidi-Corp](https://vidi-corp.com/small-business-data-analytics/); no small-business forecast case; keep it lab and freemium); standalone GA4 setup (commodity, [CSP](https://www.csp.agency/seo-services/google-analytics-4-audit-setup-services/); fold into the audit). Heads not worth chasing: honolulu seo agency ([SEO.co](https://seo.co/honolulu/)), aeo/geo agency ([Respona](https://respona.com/blog/answer-engine-optimization-agencies/)), free ai visibility checker ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/ai-visibility-checker)).
- **Hawaii signals, reported and unverified:** Hawaii Tourism Authority research said nearly two-thirds of likely Hawaii travelers use AI tools to plan ([UGA Grady Newsource](https://gradynewsource.uga.edu/tech-driven-tourism-how-ai-social-media-affect-travel-in-hawaii/)); Beat of Hawaii reports wrong AI itineraries ([article](https://beatofhawaii.com/ai-planned-seven-days-in-hawaii-then-wrecked-their-vacation/)). Whitespark, per its search summary: local packs on 93% of local-intent queries, AI Overviews on 92% of informational local ones ([Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/)), so local-intent pages compete in the pack and need a Google Business Profile.
