$126,800/$57.80 for data scientists — not the $105,650/$120,230 or $167,180/$132,380 figures being
cited to it). The $120,230/$105,650 national medians and the $167,180/$132,380 industry cross-cut
are both **OOH-sourced figures**, not Table-1 figures. The blog draft cites/links "BLS OEWS" →

section, FAQ answer #2, the last of which is inside the page's `FAQPage` JSON-LD) — a reader who

as "national A01 results." This is a body-copy citation bug, not a data-accuracy bug — the numbers
are all real and correct, only the specific hyperlink attached to them is wrong.

headline figures, including both halves of the "ranking flips inside computer systems design"

corrections, and neither should take long; combined with Mitch's still-outstanding approval, those
are the three things standing between this and done.
