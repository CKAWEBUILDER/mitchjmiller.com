## 3. Ranked fix list

### Blocking
1. **13× headline stat mislabels its own segment.** $86 is First Page Sage's **B2B** eCommerce (wholesale) figure, not B2C — confirmed by the ledger's own note, the live primary source, and the artifact's own embedded data note, all three. As written, the blog's "a B2C ecommerce customer costs $86 — a 13× gap" and the LinkedIn's "B2B education against B2C ecommerce" sentence are not describing a B2B-vs-B2C comparison at all; they're two B2B numbers from different verticals. Fix: either use shock-stat #6's own correct framing ("B2B eCommerce $86 vs. B2B Education $1,143 — same 'B2B' label, 13× spread"), swap in the real B2C ecommerce figure ($66, → 17.3×) if a true B2B-vs-B2C cross-vertical stat is wanted, or drop the 13× claim and lead only on the accurate 7.3×/1.3× same-vertical range.
2. **G2 92% citation does not support the claim.** The exact URL in the ledger, the artifact's Sources drawer, and both drafts — fetched twice, directly — contains no 92% figure and no peer-review statistic; it's a different (2026, AI-chatbot) G2 report. Fix: locate G2's actual 2024 Buyer Behavior Report (or another primary source for this figure) and re-cite with a working URL, or drop/replace the stat before publish.
3. **Standard pre-publish gates still open**, confirmed still true: `approved: no` in front-matter (house rule #6 — never publish without Mitch's sign-off); artifact not yet copied to `public/viz/b2b-vs-b2c-by-vertical/` or `public/artifacts/` (confirmed neither path exists in the repo yet), which means the blog's embed iframe and noscript poster fallback will 404 until that copy-and-repoint step runs; keyword research (Semrush or WebSearch) never run per `kw_note`.

### Optional (real, not urgent)
4. **Target-size CSS gap.** `.src` and `.note-btn` (the per-number source/note buttons — the exact mechanism that delivers "every number carries its source") measure 12–15px tall at both 1360 and 390, against the kit's own SPEC §8 bar of 38px/44px. `min-height:0` is set explicitly in the CSS. Quick fix (padding or a larger invisible hit-area) before a mobile-heavy LinkedIn push.
5. **Ruler's 7.6% is asserted as "B2B SaaS"** in blog prose twice, though Ruler's own page doesn't split B2B/C for software (and the ledger/artifact correctly tag it `overall`). Soften the wording (e.g., "the software category" rather than "B2B SaaS").
6. **LinkedIn attachment order** recommends PNG-primary/GIF-alternative, reversed from the brief's stated "GIF default, PNG fallback" expectation — confirm intent with Mitch or swap.
7. **LinkedIn honesty line is paraphrased**, not verbatim ("rudimentary version… frozen comparison" vs. "static/GIF version of a living artifact") — same substance, cosmetic only.
8. **One keyword literally repeats** across two sections (TL;DR + Acquisition-cost) against a strict one-per-section reading; the studio's own per-section table treats this as intentional.
9. Builder-flagged items carried forward unresolved: financial-services 195-day sales-cycle figure (self-disputed 2×, shown with the disagreement stated); Unbounce financial-services 8.4% landing-page figure (secondary-aggregator-only, not independently confirmed).
10. First-read friction, one line each: the 13× figure is discussed in prose but never reachable inside the artifact itself (default view opens on 7.3×, and no vertical pair reproduces "13×" as a rendered ratio), so a reader can't check it against the "living" artifact directly; the small source/note buttons (see #4) are easy to miss on first encounter, undercutting the sourcing payoff right when a curious reader would act on it; the metric dropdown mixes dollar/percent/day/count measurement types under one fixed sentence frame, so the sentence has to be mentally re-parsed on every metric switch.

## 4. Verdict

Mechanically this artifact is in excellent shape — verify (36/36), contrast (51/51), all four exports present and correctly sized, a valid multi-frame GIF89a, zero external requests, honest "No source" gap rendering, and the specific play()-wipes-merged-state bug the brief flagged is confirmed fixed across seven live selector changes including two rapid, back-to-back ones. Four of six spot-checked figures (Education CAC, Unbounce 3.8%, WordStream CPL/CPC, and Ruler's raw number) trace exactly to their ledger rows and their live primary sources. But two of the six fail on exactly the axis this piece is built to be trustworthy about: the 13× "cross-vertical" headline stat mislabels its own $86 figure as B2C when the ledger, the live First Page Sage source, and the artifact's own embedded note all agree it's a B2B (wholesale) number — meaning the flashiest stat in the post isn't actually a B2B-vs-B2C comparison at all; and the G2 92% review-reliance figure's cited URL, checked twice by direct fetch, does not contain that statistic anywhere. Both are content-level honesty failures, not style nits, in a piece whose entire thesis is "don't misread what a benchmark actually measured" — they need to be fixed, not just noted, before this goes anywhere near Mitch's approval step. Add the pre-existing publish gates (no approval yet, artifact not copied to `public/`, no keyword pass run) and a real but smaller target-size accessibility gap on the exact buttons that deliver the sourcing promise, and this is close but not done.

**Blocking fixes: (1) re-label or replace the $86/13× figure's segment claim, (2) fix or drop the unsupported G2 92% citation, (3) clear the standing pre-publish gates (approval, public/ copy, keyword pass) before this ships.**
