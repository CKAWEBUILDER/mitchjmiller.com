## Rules

- Do not publish LinkedIn modal screenshots directly.
- Crop browser chrome, LinkedIn UI, timestamps, notifications, and unrelated descriptions.
- Use 16:9 desktop crops for case-study cards: target 1408 x 768.
- Use clear labels for representative mockups: "Representative mockup - not client data."
- Do not show confidential client data.
- Prefer real public surfaces for Apple, Stanford, Dignity/CommonSpirit proof.
- Keep final assets in `public/images/` or `public/images/proof/`, then update `src/lib/images.ts`.
