# Design-first content and UX brief

September 11, 2026 · Proposal for the complete-HTML portfolio. Content/UX contribution only; no canonical source, hosting or production edits.

## The story the design should tell

**Make the complex useful.** Mitch connects data, design and engineering to create tools, experiences and growth systems. Enterprise work establishes depth; independent projects show curiosity; current client work shows delivery. Search is one substantial capability within this range.

Use a confident independent-practice voice, not a fictional multi-person agency. First person is appropriate. Avoid a wall of software names, generic service tiles, unverified active-project counts and claims that future concepts are working products.

### Recommended homepage order

1. **Positioning:** “Make the complex useful.” One explanatory paragraph and two actions: Explore the work / Start a conversation. A small resume link preserves the hiring path. The visual should express how data, experience and implementation connect, rather than illustrate search rankings alone.
2. **Experience:** Apple, CommonSpirit / Dignity Health, Stanford Health Care, Clarity AI. Label this Experience, not a current-client roster. Link into actual case studies.
3. **Selected work:** give Apple and the healthcare location platform comparable, prominent space. Follow with SFC and ClarityPulse as substantial current/product examples. Use a real image, one useful sentence, clear role/context and one properly qualified proof point for each. SFC should not monopolize the page.
4. **Capabilities through the work:** four connected disciplines—Understand, Design, Build, Grow. Data/measurement, creativity/UX, engineering/backend/data models, content/SEO/CRO appear here with links to the relevant work. Historical paid-search experience can remain attributed to Stanford; future SEM expansion is not a newly proven service claim.
5. **The lab:** working tools alongside explicitly labeled ideas. Include the UBI calculator as a named candidate whose source/status is still being reconciled, and the simulated-population workbench as a concept. Existing destination-intent, growth and content-system tools can be demonstrated now. Invite discussion of a concrete problem or idea without implying a community platform already exists.
6. **Independent work:** Date Night and verified independent projects have their own space, with honest status and working destinations. DomainSignal may retain its existing project story, but adding a lab inside DomainSignal is future scope and should not be represented as current implementation.
7. **Writing and the person:** a small editorial selection, then a brief personal introduction and hiring/resume path. Four current PDFs already exist; reuse their filenames.
8. **Inquiry:** “What are you trying to make possible?” Explain that the contact flow prepares an email for review and sending. Do not imply submissions are stored or a booking appointment is confirmed.

## Navigation and discovery

Keep top-level navigation short: **Work · How I help · Lab · Writing · About**, with **Let’s talk** as the main action and **Resumes** as a utility link. Use established routes: /work/, /collab-ideas/, /lab/, /blog/, /about/, /contact/, /resume/.

Work collection facets must remain separate:

- Context: Enterprise / Client growth & operations / Independent products.
- Capability: Data & modeling / UX & visual design / Software & data systems / AI & measurement / Content & discovery / CRO & growth.
- Status: Active / Maintained / Completed / Prototype / Concept / Unverified. Do not expose Unverified as a public product claim; resolve or omit the claim.

A case study is a story, not automatically a distinct active project. Preserve the 17 case-story URLs, but reconcile the approximately 15 active projects before declaring completeness. The nine existing Selected Builds are a partial inventory. All cards should have a real detail destination; app-store/demo buttons appear only when those destinations are verified.

## Reusable template contracts

- **Global shell:** native navigation, skip link, semantic main/footer, build-time metadata/canonical/indexing, responsive menu enhancement.
- **Collection:** title/deck, complete static card list, context/capability/status metadata, optional progressive filters and counts. Every public item and link exists before JavaScript runs.
- **Project:** name, relationship, current status/date, role, problem, actual implementation, artifacts, source-backed results and related case stories. Separate actual stack from planned components.
- **Case study:** context/role, challenge, decisions, implementation, outcomes, date/source notes and related work. Show metric definitions next to the number. Figures identify actual public interface, source capture or representative diagram.
- **Lab artifact/concept:** question, status, explanation, source/methodology, available interaction and a focused collaboration CTA. Existing tools receive functioning controls; ideas receive explanatory content, not fake Launch buttons. A concept can be useful before it becomes a product.
- **Writing:** full static article/note body, type/date/author, source links and related items. Drafts stay excluded.
- **Profile/resumes/contact:** readable HTML summaries, stable PDF downloads and honest inquiry behavior.

## Representative cases

### Apple: “From seasonal demand to enterprise execution.”

Lead with the coordination problem: multiple seasonal commerce surfaces and Americas markets require connected research, international architecture, rendering/indexation work and stakeholder alignment. Preserve the formal title **Program Manager, SEO - Americas Region (AMR)**. Use the approved program scope and 60-plus stakeholder readout scale; do not add unverified Apple revenue/lift or global-program ownership. Current public Apple imagery is context, not a screenshot of internal work.

### SFC: “Turn local knowledge into something people can explore.”

Show the complete engagement—market research, web/content architecture, interactive product, campaign/reporting and controlled release. The real aerial and ten-break explorer make the product tangible. Display 5→54 search clicks and 470→1,652 impressions with comparison dates. State concisely that the map launched September 10 after the August 12–September 8 measurement window. Do not bury the result beneath generic disclaimers or credit the new map with earlier growth.

### Lab: UBI and simulated populations

The UBI calculator is an explicitly requested portfolio candidate. Locate the actual artifact and inspect what it computes before writing functionality, model or result claims. A design review may include a clearly labeled proposed showcase, with no invented live URL or nonworking Run button.

The simulated-population workbench remains a concept: define a decision, select suitable public data, inspect assumptions and compare scenarios. Proposed CRM import, model accuracy and saved experiments are not current capabilities. Source, date, model assumptions and observed-versus-simulated values belong in the future template.

The public lab may share ideas and demonstrations without publishing repository source. Browser-delivered HTML/JavaScript is still inspectable. Keep proprietary backend code and private data separate; no client uploads, authentication or community platform are implied by this design.

## Design-review decisions

Confirm the theme using homepage, Apple/SFC case treatment, a writing page and one working island before mass conversion. Agree on type, image treatment, enterprise/current-project balance and the status labels. The proposed content JSON separates public copy, verified evidence and pending concepts so the implementation does not accidentally turn an internal instruction into visitor copy.

Reuse the current narrative, resume and interaction work. No new asset generation is necessary to settle content hierarchy. Root owns design integration, browser review, the persistent content schema and the new staging build. Production follows the user’s later approval.
