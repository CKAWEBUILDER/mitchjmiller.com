The useful distinction

Origin is not destination.

“Waikīkī” names a market of interest. “Near me” asks the search system to use location context. These signals imply different customer needs, even when the service is the same.

The strategy

Help people discover, then decide.

Useful pages answer planning and service-choice questions. A complete, consistent local presence helps a person evaluate the business. Build the connection between the two.
