Process

Start small. See clearly. Move forward.

Clear priorities. Shared progress. A useful next step.

  1. Define the question.

    Agree what matters, who it serves and what success would mean.

  2. Find the evidence.

    Inspect the data, constraints and assumptions shaping the decision.

  3. Make it useful.

    Review the strategy, content or software as it develops.

  4. Learn from the result.

    Measure what changed and decide what deserves attention next.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who will I work with?

+

Mitchell Miller leads the work directly, from research and strategy through hands-on delivery. The scope determines any additional specialist support.

What does an engagement cost?

+

Cost follows the question, scope and deliverables. Start with a conversation; agree the work before it begins.

Do you work with smaller businesses?

+

Yes. The work spans enterprise programs and independent businesses, with the approach shaped to the opportunity.

Is there a free way to explore the approach?

+

Yes. The learning lab offers interactive tools for exploring data, testing assumptions and comparing scenarios. Explore the lab.

How are results measured?

+

Agree the baseline and success measures first. Reports connect each result to its source, time period and limits. The case studies show that discipline in practice.

Can you work with our existing team?

+

Yes. Work can complement your engineering, design, marketing or analytics team through shared priorities, clear requirements and practical handoffs.
