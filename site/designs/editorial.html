Inside the work / Case preview

A thousand locations.
One system for the next patient action.

DIGNITY HEALTH / COMMONSPIRIT
2018–2019 BUILD
PRODUCT MANAGEMENT · ENTITY ARCHITECTURE · ANALYTICS

Back to selected work ↑

The challenge

Nine service areas operated like separate web experiences. Location information, templates and paths to care varied by market. A patient could find a facility and still face unnecessary work to act.

The decision

Put the entity model before the page template. I drove a location system with SEO, design, analytics, data owners, regional stakeholders and Yext engineering—connecting governed information to reusable experiences.

The system

More than 1,000 location pages connected discovery to calls, directions and appointment pathways. Measurement was part of the requirements, so the product could be assessed through patient actions.

A good template is only as useful as the system beneath it.
